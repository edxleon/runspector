The app speaks plainly now: today's reasons as sentences and one line on what to do, every technical word with an ⓘ that explains it. You can check the coach: every point on a card says where it comes from, and the card shows what the AI read for it. And the coach sees how your runs actually went - easy, moderate or hard, by your own heart-rate zones - over the last ten runs and since your first.

```bash
uv tool upgrade runspector      # or, without uv: pipx upgrade runspector
runspector serve
```

**Plain words**
- Today's reasons read as sentences - "You slept badly (sleep score 41 of 100)", "Your resting heart rate is 6 beats above your normal" - with one line on what to do: "Today: no training. Rest - a walk is fine." On a green day that still says "easy", the line says why (the last hard session was too recent, the week already has its share). The technical reasons are under Details.
- An ⓘ beside HRV, resting heart rate, Body Battery, the load ratio (ACWR), VO2max, the heart-rate zones, load and more explains each in a sentence or two.
- One word for each kind of run - easy, moderate, hard - in the run list, the You tab and the coach's answers; time in heart-rate zones is named by its zone, since the easy limit the app sets can reach into zone 3.
- The You tab says each thing the coach has learned in a plain sentence; what the coach reads is under Details.
- Fixed: a coach box that read "The last card is from – —", a chart legend cut off at "line =", "28 days – · 56 days no baseline" under VO2max, and the resting heart rate shown as +7 in one place and +6 in another.
- "Tap again" waits ten seconds and is no longer reset by the job list's redraw while a coach run is going - a first tap that seemed to do nothing. "Really cancel?" stays armed through the redraw too.
- A failed coach run says what to do ("Try again", "Your Claude usage limit is reached"); the technical reason is in its log. The model's name is no longer on every card, and the number check reads "52 of 53 numbers found in your data".

- A coach card says each thing once: the headline the answer, the conclusion under it - whole now, it was cut at 160 characters - only what to do next, the points the numbers behind it. Points come from your data unless marked "you said" or "coach's reading"; the check of every number is in "How this card was made". What changed since the last card sits with the further points.
- The You tab's week is "Your usual week", Cancel sits where Edit was, and the long-run day no longer wears the ring that means "today".

**How a card was made**
- Every point on a coach card is marked: your data (and which tool it came from), what you said, or the coach's own reading. A number the coach worked out itself - an easy cap of 0.83 x your zone-5 floor, say - is marked as worked out, not passed off as measured.
- "How this card was made" under each card lists every tool the coach called, what came back, and a check of every number on the card against it: found in the data, or not. The check is literal and says so - a number found was in front of the coach, it does not prove it was used rightly.
- The You tab says in plain words what the AI reads, what the app keeps of it, where and for how long, and lists the latest steps of your chats in Claude Code or Claude Desktop. Everything stays on this computer under `~/.runspector/evidence/`: a card's record lives as long as the card, a chat's steps 14 days.
- A card run no longer leaves a copy of its session with Claude Code.

**How your runs went**
- The sync now fetches zones and laps for every run of the last half year, 20 a sync, recent runs first - so after a few syncs your whole history has them, not just the last five weeks.
- Each run is read by its own zone time: easy, moderate or hard. A run with workout steps is read by its steps (reps that never got hard are moderate, whatever the warm-up did to the average), and a run held within the easy band the app puts on your watch is easy. The run list colours each run the same way and says why.
- The You tab, the coach's cards and the `get_athlete_profile` tool get the count: "last 10: mostly moderate - 2 easy, 8 moderate", or "easy and hard kept apart", and beside it how your first runs read when they read differently. When Garmin moved your zones in between, the line says so.
- Garmin's "Quality" and "Long Run" labels come from the training effect and a run's length; the coach no longer takes them for how a run was run.
- Runs whose zone 4 sits far below the heart rate you reach are left out of the count, with how to fix the zones in Garmin Connect.
- `get_intensity_distribution` says when zone 3 starts below the app's own easy cap (with Garmin's %-of-max zones): easy runs held near the cap count as zone 3 there. The easy-pace trend takes the runs the list calls easy.
- The coach's reference now says the classic mistake is no spread - every run moderate - not zone 3 itself: for recreational runners a pyramidal week does as well as a polarised one.

**Fixes**
- "Analyze this run" on an older run fetches its zones and laps from Garmin first, instead of analysing a run without them; in a chat, `analyze_workout` does the same.
- A card whose answer the model closed with a wrong bracket is no longer lost.
- A running job's clock ticks every second.
- One run Garmin answers with "not found" no longer stops the sync's detail fetch.

**Under the hood**
- No database change; the MCP server keeps its 17 tools.
- `docs/roadmap.md` has two new items first among the open ones: an app set up and started without a terminal (24), and the page and the coach in your language (23).
- The simulator has five runners with typical habits - every run moderate, easy runs too hard, reps that never get hard, one who gets better, one held at the cap - checked on every day of the last two weeks; one new eval case.
