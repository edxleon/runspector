The planned week is yours to change: tap a day to see what a session is and move it, shorten it, swap it or take it out - or tell the coach in your own words. You decide; the coach says what it thinks of it.

```bash
uv tool upgrade runspector      # or, without uv: pipx upgrade runspector
runspector serve
```

**A day of the week, opened**
- Tap a day in the Today tab's week strip: what the session is (an easy run you can talk through, threshold, VO2max intervals ...), its minutes, the distance and average pace it comes to, the steps of a hard one, and the easier option it carries. Hovering a day says it in one line.
- Change it right there: move it to another day of the next two weeks, give it another length or kind, take it out, or add a run on an empty day. A workout from your own Garmin calendar (not one the app made) can be taken out or replaced with one of the app's.
- Or tell the coach under the strip - "Friday instead of Saturday, and Sunday's long run only 60 minutes". The coach reads your calendar with each day's weekday and date, so "Saturday" is the Saturday you mean, and files exactly the changes you asked for.

**You decide, the coach comments**
- Every change is kept as you made it. Beside the button the coach says what it thinks: more running than the plan would give this week (and by how much), two hard days within 48 hours (the long run counts), more than two hard sessions, a hard session in a return week, the week's last hard session gone - and which one it was -, a run on a day you said you are ill or in pain, on today's Rest, on a day you cannot run, longer than any session should be now, or on a day that already holds one.
- Nothing reaches your watch before "Put on watch" (two taps), and "Undo the change" puts your calendar back as it was - also when the change only took a session off.

**For Claude Code and Claude Desktop**
- A new MCP tool, `propose_change` (18 tools now): the athlete's own change to the week as a proposal with the coach's notes. `get_training_readiness` lists the next 14 calendar days, each with its weekday, date and schedule id.

**Under the hood**
- One path for the tap and the words: `plan.propose_change` builds the proposal, `plan.change_notes` the coach's word on it - the same rules the planner builds a week with.
- Two new conversation stories with the real coach: a change in German words, and an ambitious change kept with the concern said.
- No database change.
