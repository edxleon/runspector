A coach card is a conversation now: ask on under it, and every answer knows everything asked and answered before. A card can offer the next step as a button. And the coach keeps what you tell it about yourself - your time on weekdays, your goal, an old injury - checked against your own words before it is kept, shown to you, and let go with one tap.

```bash
uv tool upgrade runspector      # or, without uv: pipx upgrade runspector
runspector serve
```

**A card is a conversation**
- Ask on under a card or under any answer in it: the answer is written with the card and every question and answer before it, each with the day it was asked - "my calf twinges" from Monday is not Thursday's state. A sixth question still has your first one in front of the coach.
- One field to ask on stands under the whole conversation. While an answer is on its way the next question waits for it, so no answer is written without the one before.
- A card may offer up to two next steps - plan the week, should I train today, review the week, why VO2max moves - as a button (two taps) that starts that card with the conversation as its context. Nothing reaches your watch without the yes it always needs.
- A conversation keeps up to 12 answers; then a new question is clearer.

**What you tell the coach**
- Say something lasting about yourself while asking - "on weekdays I only have 40 minutes before work", "my left knee does not like long downhills", "my goal is a half marathon in April" - also inside a question (measured in English and German). The coach notes it in a few words, and a second check reads your message and keeps the note only if you said it, with its meaning: a "not" stays a "not", what you are unsure about stays unsure, what you only ask about is no fact, a one-off this Saturday is not lasting, and someone else's habit is not yours. Any failure keeps nothing.
- The card says "Noted for later" with a Forget beside it. The You tab lists every fact with the question it came from and lets each go; the coach reads them before every answer and in chats (`get_athlete_profile`).
- When something changes - "from next week only 30 minutes" - the new fact takes the old one's place and the card says what it replaced. A list for the coach holds 24 facts; when it is full the card says so and nothing old is pushed out.
- Forget takes the fact out of everything the coach is given from then on, also the stored records of earlier cards and chats. A conversation where you said it keeps your words until you delete that card.
- A pain or a cold is never kept as a fact: when you mention one, the card offers the Today tab's check-in (Pain, Ill, Sore legs, Stressed) as a button, so the next conversation knows it too - your tap says it, never the coach.

**Fixes**
- An answer in a conversation whose first card's job record was cleaned up (after 14 days or 50 runs) refused every question.
- "You asked" shows your words as you typed them: "5*3 min" no longer turns into "53 min".
- A question sent while the previous answer was still on its way was answered without it.

**Under the hood**
- The second check runs on `sonnet` with no tools and nothing but your message and your earlier questions in the conversation; it adds a few seconds to a card that keeps a fact. Measured on its own cases, three repeats each, haiku got 116-123 of 129 verdicts right and changed its mind between runs; sonnet, with a note required to be about you in every word, 132 of 132 (every run of every model: `evals/MEMORY_CHECK_LOG.md`).
- Two new eval suites with the real model, run weekly: the check before a fact is kept (`evals/memory_check.yaml`) and five conversations end to end against the demo athlete (`evals/conversations.yaml`). A property test runs the bookkeeping of kept facts over long random courses.
- The release gate also reads the text of every commit before it is published.
- No database change; the MCP server keeps its 17 tools.
