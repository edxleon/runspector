Your own word counts: say you are ill, in pain, sore or stressed, and the app goes by it - today's call, the week it plans and every session it proposes - until you say it is over.

```bash
uv tool upgrade runspector      # or, without uv: pipx upgrade runspector
runspector serve
```

**Check-ins**
- Four buttons under today's call: Ill, Pain, Sore legs, Stressed - and a `check_in` tool for the chat ("I've caught a cold" in Claude Code or Claude Desktop records it). The watch sees a cold only once your HRV reacts; until then it would have offered a hard day.
- Ill or pain rest the day whatever the watch says, also before the morning's sync. Once it is over, as many easy days follow as it held, at most seven.
- Sore legs or stress take the hard session, not the run.
- A check-in is a state, not a day: it holds until you say "It's over", or runs out three days after you last said it (sore legs: two). Saying it again keeps the same episode and its first day.
- A week planned while one is open is not stepped up and holds no hard session before the earliest day one could come back; a single hard session proposed for such a day is refused. Both planners ask the same rule, and a test holds that a check-in never makes a week bigger.
- A tap made by mistake can be taken back the same day: tap it again, or "Said by mistake". "It's over" on the day you said it keeps today and ends tomorrow.
- The coach's automatic cards can neither record nor end a check-in - only you can, on the page or in the chat. A card written before a check-in is marked out of date.

**The Today tab**
- On a day your own word decides, the call says so first: what you said, then the buttons, then the watch as context. A line under the buttons says what a tap does before you tap.
- Each open state says when it runs out, with plain buttons: Still there, Gone, Undo.
- The week strip and "Coming up" mark the sessions a check-in holds back - "not today" or "on hold" - with when hard sessions may come back.

**Under the hood**
- The database moves to schema 6 on the first start (a `check_ins` table); nothing else changes.
- The MCP server has 17 tools (`check_in` is new).
- The simulator has a runner whose calf hurts after one quality session in seven; two new eval cases: a cold said in chat means no run today, and a cold written only in the note sends you to the Ill button.
- For contributors: CONTRIBUTING.md and the `/deep-review` skill in `.claude/skills/` say which changes get a deep review before the commit and what it is.
- `docs/roadmap.md` is reordered by what each item does for the runner, with seven new items.
